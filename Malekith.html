Maliketh is Queen Marika's shadow, a beast guardian created to serve and protect her. He guards Destined Death, the power that can truly kill gods and demigods, making him one of the most important figures in the story.
After a piece of the Rune of Death was stolen during the Night of Black Knives, Maliketh sealed the remaining power inside his own body. His Black Blade is terrifying because it carries the power of true death.
