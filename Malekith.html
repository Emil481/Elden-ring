Whats the background of Malekith
what connection does malekith have to the other demigods
Maliketh is Queen Marika's shadow, a beast guardian created to serve her. He is trusted with Destined Death, the power that can truly kill gods and demigods. This makes him one of the most important characters in the story, because whoever controls Destined Death can change the fate of the world.
During the Night of Black Knives, part of the Rune of Death was stolen from Maliketh and used to kill Godwyn the Golden. Because of this failure, Maliketh sealed the remaining Rune of Death inside his own body. His Black Blade carries that power, which is why the fight feels so dangerous. Defeating him releases Destined Death back into the world.
