Who is Margit the fell
Why is Margit guarding Stormveil
Margit is an Omen, a cursed and outcast being born with horns. Omens are usually hidden away or killed at birth, but Margit survived and became a powerful warrior.
Margit guards Stormveil to stop weak Tarnished from reaching the demigods and the Erdtree. He tests anyone trying to become Elden Lord.
