Who is Radahn
Why is Radahn called Starscourge
Radahn is the son of Radagon and Rennala, and he is sibling to Ranni and Rykard. He was a legendary warrior who admired Godfrey, the first Elden Lord.
Radahn is called Starscourge because his gravity magic held the stars in place. Even after being infected by scarlet rot, his power kept the sky locked.
