Rykard, lord of blasphemy
Whats Rykard's story
Why did Rykard join the serpent
Rykard was once a noble demigod, but he rejected the Golden Order. He became Lord of Blasphemy and led a rebellion from Volcano Manor.
Rykard fused with the god-devouring serpent because he wanted immortality and enough power to challenge the gods themselves.
