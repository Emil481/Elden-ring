Morgott the omen king
Why did Morgott come back
Why is his name changed
Morgott returned to defend Leyndell and the Erdtree. Even though the Golden Order rejected him for being an Omen, he still chose to protect it.
Morgott used the name Margit as a disguise. It let him fight Tarnished early without revealing that he was the hidden king of Leyndell.
