Morgott the omen king
Why did Morgott come back
Why is his name changed
Morgott is the true identity behind Margit. He is an Omen and a child of the royal family, but because Omens are hated by the Golden Order, he was hidden and rejected instead of celebrated.
Even though the Golden Order treated him badly, Morgott still defends Leyndell and the Erdtree. That makes him tragic: he protects a system that never accepted him, because he believes the throne must not fall to an unworthy Tarnished.
