Morgott the omen king
Why did Morgott come back
Why is his name changed
Morgott is the true identity behind Margit. He is an Omen born into the royal family, but Omens are hated by the Golden Order, so he was hidden away instead of being honored as a prince. Even after being rejected, he becomes the secret king of Leyndell and protects the capital from anyone trying to take the throne.
Morgott is tragic because he defends the same order that treated him like a curse. He believes the other demigods are traitors and that the Tarnished is unworthy of becoming Elden Lord. His fight is more personal than Margit because this time he reveals who he truly is. He stands as the last loyal guardian of Leyndell before the path to the Erdtree opens.
