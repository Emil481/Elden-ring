Godrick the grafted
Who is Godrick the grafted
Why does Godrick graft body parts
Godrick is a demigod and a distant descendant of Queen Marika, but his divine blood is weak. He rules Stormveil Castle, yet he is mocked by stronger demigods because he is cowardly, insecure, and desperate to prove himself.
Godrick grafts extra arms and body parts onto himself to steal strength from other warriors. His dragon arm in the second phase shows how far he will go for power, even if it makes him look monstrous.
