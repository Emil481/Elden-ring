Godrick the grafted
Who is Godrick the grafted
Why does Godrick graft body parts
Godrick is a demigod from the royal family of Queen Marika. His bloodline is weak compared to the other demigods, and many see him as a disgrace.
Godrick grafts limbs onto himself because he wants more power. He uses the strength of others to hide how weak and desperate he really is.
