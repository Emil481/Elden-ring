Godrick the grafted
Who is Godrick the grafted
Why does Godrick graft body parts
Godrick is a demigod and a distant descendant of Queen Marika, but his divine blood is much weaker than the other shardbearers. He rules Stormveil Castle, but he is insecure and desperate for respect. Other demigods see him as pathetic, so he tries to look powerful by surrounding himself with soldiers and stolen body parts.
Godrick grafts limbs onto himself because he wants strength he was not born with. Grafting means attaching body parts from other warriors to his own body. In phase two, he cuts off his own arm and grafts a dragon head onto it, showing how far he will go for power. He is important because he is usually the first Great Rune boss the player defeats.
