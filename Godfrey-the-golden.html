Godfrey the golden
Why is Godfrey the golden in Elden Ring
What does his spirit protect
Godfrey the Golden is a golden spirit version of Godfrey, the first Elden Lord. This version appears in Leyndell like a memory of the old Golden Order. He represents the age when Godfrey served Queen Marika and conquered enemies for the Erdtree. The fight gives the player a preview of Godfrey before meeting his real body later.
His spirit guards the path deeper into the capital and tests whether the Tarnished deserves to continue. Even though this version is not the real Godfrey at full strength, he still shows the power and dignity of the first Elden Lord. It also reminds the player that the throne has a long history before they try to claim it.
