Rykard, lord of blasphemy
Whats Rykard's story
Why did Rykard join the serpent
Rykard is one of the demigod children of Radagon and Rennala. Instead of serving the Golden Order, he became the lord of Volcano Manor and built a rebellion against the Erdtree and the gods.
He allowed himself to be eaten by the God-Devouring Serpent so they could become one being. Rykard wanted immortality and the power to devour the gods, but his rebellion turned him into something horrifying.
