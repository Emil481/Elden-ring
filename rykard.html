Rykard, lord of blasphemy
Whats Rykard's story
Why did Rykard join the serpent
Rykard is one of the children of Radagon and Rennala, and he became the lord of Volcano Manor. Unlike many others, he openly rejected the Golden Order and started a rebellion against the Erdtree. His followers hunt Tarnished, and Volcano Manor becomes a place for people who want to fight against the current order.
Rykard let himself be devoured by the God-Devouring Serpent so they could become one being. He wanted immortality and enough power to devour the gods themselves. His boss fight is strange because the player is meant to use the Serpent-Hunter weapon. Rykard shows what rebellion can become when it turns into obsession and hunger for power.
