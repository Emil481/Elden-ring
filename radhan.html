Starscourge Radahn
Who is Radahn
Why is Radahn called Starscourge
Radahn is the son of Radagon and Rennala, and the brother of Ranni and Rykard. He admired Godfrey so much that he became a huge battlefield warrior with a red mane and lion-like style. He learned gravity magic from Sellia, not only for battle, but also so he could keep riding his small horse Leonard even after becoming enormous.
Radahn is called Starscourge because he used gravity magic to hold back the stars themselves. During the war after the Shattering, he fought Malenia and was infected with scarlet rot. By the time the player finds him, his mind is mostly gone, so the Radahn Festival is held to give him an honorable warrior death. His death also releases the stars and opens the way to Nokron.
