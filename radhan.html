Starscourge Radahn
Who is Radahn
Why is Radahn called Starscourge
Radahn is the son of Radagon and Rennala, and the brother of Ranni and Rykard. He admired Godfrey so much that he shaped himself into a huge battlefield warrior and became one of the strongest demigods.
Radahn learned gravity magic so he could keep riding his small horse, Leonard, even after becoming massive. His power was so great that he held back the stars, and his fight happens during a festival made to finally give him an honorable death.
