Lore about Elden Ring

The Lands Between

The world is ruled by the power of the Elden Ring and the Erdtree. After the Elden Ring shattered, the demigods fought for its pieces.

The Demigods

The demigods are powerful children or relatives of Queen Marika and Radagon. Many of them hold Great Runes and became major bosses.

The Tarnished

The player is a Tarnished, called back to the Lands Between to gather Great Runes and try to become Elden Lord.
