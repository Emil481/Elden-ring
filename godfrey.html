Before becoming Elden Lord, Godfrey was Hoarah Loux, a brutal warrior from the Badlands. Queen Marika chose him as her consort, and he became the first Elden Lord after leading wars for the Golden Order.
Godfrey fights the Tarnished as a final test before the throne. When he becomes Hoarah Loux again, he stops acting like a noble lord and returns to his original warrior nature, fighting with raw strength.
