Who is Godfrey

Before he became Elden Lord, Godfrey was Hoarah Loux, a brutal warrior from the Badlands. Queen Marika chose him as her consort and made him the first Elden Lord. To control his violent nature, he carried the beast regent Serosh on his back. As Godfrey, he led wars for the Golden Order and became a symbol of strength.
Godfrey fights the Tarnished near the end of the game as a final test before the throne. In the second phase, he casts away Serosh and becomes Hoarah Loux again, fighting with pure strength instead of royal dignity. This shows who he really was before the Golden Order shaped him. His battle asks whether the Tarnished is strong enough to replace the first Elden Lord.
