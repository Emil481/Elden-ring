Basic info about Elden Ring

What is Elden Ring?

Elden Ring is an open world action RPG made by FromSoftware. You play as a Tarnished exploring the Lands Between, fighting bosses and collecting Great Runes.

How do you progress?

You explore areas, level up with runes, upgrade weapons, defeat major bosses, and move closer to becoming Elden Lord.

Why is it hard?

The game expects you to learn enemy attacks, dodge at the right time, and use your build wisely. If something is too hard, exploring somewhere else usually helps.
