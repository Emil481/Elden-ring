Fire giant
Whats Fire Giant's background
Why does he guard the flame
The Fire Giant is the last survivor of the Fire Giants, an ancient race from the Mountaintops of the Giants. His people worshipped the Fell God and guarded a flame strong enough to burn the Erdtree. Long before the player arrives, Queen Marika defeated the giants because their flame was a danger to the Golden Order.
Marika cursed the last Fire Giant to guard the Forge of the Giants forever. This makes him more than just a big monster; he is a prisoner forced to protect the flame after his people were destroyed. The player must defeat him because the Forge is needed to burn the Erdtree and continue the story. His second phase shows the power of the Fell God awakening through him.
