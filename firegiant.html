Fire giant
Whats Fire Giant's background
Why does he guard the flame
The Fire Giant is the last survivor of the Fire Giants, an ancient race from the Mountaintops of the Giants. His people worshipped the Fell God and protected a flame powerful enough to burn the Erdtree.
Queen Marika defeated the giants but kept the last one alive as a curse. He is forced to guard the Forge of the Giants forever, which is why he stands in the way before the player can burn the Erdtree.
