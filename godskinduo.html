Godskin duo
Who and what are the godskin duo
What is the black flame
The Godskin Duo is made of a Godskin Apostle and a Godskin Noble. They belong to the Godskin cult, a group connected to the Gloam-Eyed Queen. Their name comes from their armor, which is made from the skin of gods or divine beings. They are some of the strangest and darkest enemies in Elden Ring.
The duo uses Black Flame, a type of fire connected to the Rune of Death. Before Destined Death was sealed away, Black Flame could kill even gods. The fight is difficult because the Apostle and Noble keep returning until the shared boss health bar is empty. Lore-wise, they show that there were once powers strong enough to threaten the gods before Marika changed the rules of death.
