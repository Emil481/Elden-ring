Godskin duo
Who and what are the godskin duo
What is the black flame
The Godskin Duo is made of a Godskin Apostle and a Godskin Noble. They belonged to a cult that served the Gloam-Eyed Queen, and their purpose was to hunt gods using the power of Black Flame.
Black Flame is connected to the Rune of Death. Before Destined Death was sealed away, this flame could kill even divine beings. Their fight is difficult because the duo can revive until the shared boss health bar is gone.
