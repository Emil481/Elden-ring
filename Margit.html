Who is Margit the fell
Why is Margit guarding Stormveil
Margit is an Omen, a cursed person born with horns. In the Golden Order, Omens are seen as shameful and dangerous, so many are locked away under the capital. Margit is secretly connected to Morgott, and he uses this name when he appears outside Leyndell.
He guards Stormveil because he wants to stop weak Tarnished before they can reach the demigods. Margit works like an early test: if the player cannot defeat him, they are not ready to challenge the stronger rulers of the Lands Between.
