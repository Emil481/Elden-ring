Who is Margit the fell
Why is Margit guarding Stormveil
Margit is an Omen, a cursed person born with horns. In the Golden Order, Omens are seen as shameful and dangerous, so many are locked away under Leyndell. Margit is not just a random guard; he is secretly Morgott using another name. He appears early to stop the Tarnished before they become a real threat.
Margit guards Stormveil because Godrick is one of the shardbearers, and the path through Stormveil leads the Tarnished closer to the Great Runes. His fight teaches the player patience, delayed attacks, and timing. Lore-wise, he is testing whether the Tarnished is strong enough to challenge the rulers of the Lands Between.
